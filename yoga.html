Yoga & Wellness

Certified Yoga Instructor · 200-hour International Certification

Outside of tech, yoga is where I slow down. What started as a personal practice for balance and focus grew into something I wanted to share — a 200-hour international certification later, I now guide others through the same sense of calm and presence I found on the mat.

I believe yoga isn't about touching your toes or holding the perfect pose — it's about building a quieter, steadier relationship with yourself, one breath at a time. That's the practice I try to bring into every session.
