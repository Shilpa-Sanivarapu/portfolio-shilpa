About Me

Hi, I'm Shilpa Sanivarapu — a B.Tech AI student who loves combining technology with creativity. I'm passionate about understanding people and using data, design, and marketing to make a difference. From building AI models to managing social media, I thrive in spaces where logic meets storytelling.

As Associate President (Environment Department) at StreetCause NGO, I coordinate with partner organizations and lead volunteer teams on sustainability initiatives — from identifying needs to seeing projects through to execution. Alongside that, I've worked as a social media manager, handling content strategy and tracking engagement to make sure the right message reached the right audience. Both roles taught me a lot about communication, ownership, and working with people toward a shared goal.

Outside tech, I enjoy painting, yoga, design, art, singing, and playing instruments — I love creating in pretty much any form I can. My goal is to grow into a product marketer who bridges innovation and empathy — making technology feel human.
